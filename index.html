About Us

Project L.A.N.C.E is a mil-sim faction based on the Roblox game BRM5

Founded by 20Buck$, we're a PMC that started off as an idea after our leader's discharge from a major faction. He founded L.A.N.C.E to provide safety to multiple locations worldwide. Two of them being of special interest: Ronograd and Sawang.

Every member enters as a recruit and goes through a rigorous selection period where they learn our foundations: core values, order of battle, internal terminology, weapons handling, tactical movement and radio usage. Only after succeeding in the final evaluation do they recieve their asignation to a division, and a proper callsing.

The chain of command is strict and respected 24/7. The squad leaders answer to Operation Officers, whom depend on central command. This structure allows us to coordinate fast deployments with a considerable number of operators without losing organization.

A contract is a mission that the faction accepts and planifies. It can consist on securing a compound, attacking a convoy, rescuing a VIP or provide cover to allies.Before each deployment there is a briefing session in which we study the map, assert routes and define extraction points.

In the aftermath of every operation, a joint revision is made. We analyze what worked out, what went wrong and what needs to be fixed, and the results are logged in for new squadrons to improve over that. This continuous improvement culture is what makes our company stand out of other factions.

El respeto es una regla innegociable. La compañía no tolera el acoso, las trampas ni el abuso de las mecánicas del juego, y exige a todos sus miembros un trato correcto dentro y fuera de las partidas. Quien no cumple las normas es sancionado o expulsado sin excepciones.Respect is a non-negotiable. We do not tolerate harrasment, cheating or exploiting, and demand every single one of our members a grounded behavior in and outside our operations. Those who do not comply to our standards shall be warned or banned with no exceptions.

Looking forward, Project L.A.N.C.E seeks to grow without losing its identity: more specialized divisions, open trainings for newcomers and joint events with allied factions. Our main goal is to ensure every Lancer feels part of something organized, serious and fun alike.
